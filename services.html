Affordable Website Design for Small Businesses

I build affordable, responsive websites for individuals, sole traders and small businesses. Whether you need a simple online presence or a multi-page business website, I aim to deliver a professional result without the cost of a large agency.

A more tailored website of up to eight pages, with bespoke layouts, styling and visual elements. Three rounds of design and content corrections. Estimated delivery: 3–4 weekends.

Every package includes the agreed website files, a defined allowance for design corrections, and free correction of defects that prevent the finished website from meeting our agreed specification.

Prices are introductory and may change for new projects.

What’s included

  • Responsive design across common screen sizes
  • Basic search-engine metadata and accessibility checks
  • Source files, handover notes and deployment guidance
  • Correction rounds: one consolidated list of changes from you counts as one round
  • 30-day period after handover to report defects; reported defects stay covered until resolved

No hosting lock-in

I supply the completed website package rather than selling hosting subscriptions. You receive your website files and can choose your own hosting provider and domain registrar. I can guide you towards suitable hosting options and explain how to deploy your website.

Any hosting, domain registration or third-party service charges are paid by you directly to the relevant provider. Hosting outages, domain configuration, third-party service failures and changes made to the code by others are outside my responsibility.

A personal, weekend-based service

I work on projects primarily at weekends, which allows me to offer affordable prices while keeping my commitments manageable. Delivery estimates are agreed before work begins, and I take on a limited number of projects at a time.

A deposit is requested before work begins, and the page list, content and broad design direction are approved in writing first.

A clear scope, with no surprises

Your quote will specify the number of pages, included features, correction rounds and expected delivery window. Additional pages, new functionality, a complete redesign or changes to an approved direction can be quoted separately.
