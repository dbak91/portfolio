Hosting options and ongoing service

You can choose and manage hosting with a provider yourself, or ask whether your website is suitable for hosting through my portal. Portal hosting may be available as a separate ongoing service, priced transparently from audited hosting and infrastructure costs plus an agreed percentage service fee for managing and maintaining the service. I’ll confirm availability, scope and the rate with you before you commit.

  • Your own domain: domain names can cost as little as £2 for an introductory period. Prices depend on the domain ending and provider, and renewal prices can be higher.
  • Provider-hosted websites: I can help you compare suitable options and guide you through setup. You keep control of your hosting account and pay the provider directly.

For portal hosting, the agreed service terms will explain what management and maintenance include, how ongoing charges are calculated, and how support and provider outages are handled. Hosting depends on suitability and availability and is not included in the website packages. Never send me your passwords.

Business and Premium packages can include one booking system or hosted payment portal. These use a third-party provider’s booking pages or hosted checkout, so I do not build a payment backend or handle card details. The provider account, any transaction or subscription fees, and service terms remain the client’s responsibility. We will agree the provider and confirm the integration is suitable before work begins.

  • Provider costs: when you arrange hosting directly, you pay hosting, domain registration and third-party service charges to the relevant provider. If portal hosting is agreed, its ongoing rate and included services will be set out separately.
  • Ongoing responsibility: support and maintenance responsibilities depend on the hosting arrangement and agreed service terms; changes made to the code by others remain outside the website package.

Corrections, fixes and changes

  • Included corrections: the stated number of rounds in which you can request reasonable changes to text, colours, images, spacing or other agreed design details.
  • What counts as a round: one consolidated list of changes from you, not one individual message or change.
  • Additional changes: new pages, new features, a complete redesign or changes to an approved direction are quoted separately.
  • Fix period: a 30-day period after handover for reporting defects. Any defect reported within that period remains covered until resolved, provided it is within the agreed scope.
  • Scope changes: written agreement and an additional quote.
  • Support: a defined defect-fix period; ongoing maintenance is sold separately.
