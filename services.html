Hosting and domains: be clear from the start

Supplying a website without hosting is a perfectly reasonable model, especially for static websites. I do not sell, resell or manage hosting or domains.

  • Hosting: client-owned and paid directly to the provider.
  • Domain: registered by you with a registrar of your choice, in your name.
  • Guidance: I can point you towards suitable hosting options and explain how to deploy the website.
  • Costs: any hosting, domain registration or third-party service charges are paid by you directly to the relevant provider.
  • Not my responsibility: hosting outages, domain configuration, third-party service failures and changes made to the code by others.

Corrections, fixes and changes

  • Included corrections: the stated number of rounds in which you can request reasonable changes to text, colours, images, spacing or other agreed design details.
  • What counts as a round: one consolidated list of changes from you, not one individual message or change.
  • Additional changes: new pages, new features, a complete redesign or changes to an approved direction are quoted separately.
  • Fix period: a 30-day period after handover for reporting defects. Any defect reported within that period remains covered until resolved, provided it is within the agreed scope.
  • Scope changes: written agreement and an additional quote.
  • Support: a defined defect-fix period; ongoing maintenance is sold separately.

A personal, weekend-based service

I work on projects primarily at weekends, which allows me to offer affordable prices while keeping my commitments manageable. Delivery estimates are agreed before work begins, and I take on a limited number of projects at a time.

For new projects I ask for a deposit before beginning work, and the page list, content and broad design direction are approved in writing first. I do not start new features just because they seem like small requests.

A clear scope, with no surprises

Your quote will specify the number of pages, included features, correction rounds and expected delivery window. Additional pages, new functionality and substantial changes to the original specification can be quoted separately.
